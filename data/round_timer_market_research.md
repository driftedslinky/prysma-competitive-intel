## 3. PRICING STRATEGY

### Competitor Pricing Summary

| App | Model | Price Point |
|-----|-------|-------------|
| Interval Timer (dreamspark) | Free + ads | No paid tier |
| Tabata Timer (evgeniysharafan) | Free + ads | No paid tier |
| Seconds Interval Timer | Freemium | $4.99 one-time (unlimited) |
| Intervals Pro | Freemium | $9.99 one-time OR $4.99/month OR $29.99/year |
| Interval Timer HIIT Timer (orosda) | Freemium | $3.99/month OR $19.99/year |
| Box Timer | Free (donation) | No paid tier |
| SmartWOD Timer | Freemium | $9.99/month OR $89.99 lifetime |
| MMA Timer Pro | Paid | $2.99 one-time |

### Pricing Recommendation

**Recommended: $4.99 one-time purchase**

**Rationale:**

1. **Market gap:** The most successful pure timer (Seconds) charges $4.99. Box Timer is free (donation). The $2.99–$4.99 range is the sweet spot for one-time purchase in this category.

2. **User sentiment:** Reddit and App Store reviews consistently praise one-time purchases and rage against subscriptions for utility apps. Quote from Seconds Pro review: "I basically rule out any app that is a subscription because if I like the app then I can't imagine paying a subscription for it indefinitely."

3. **Conversion optimization:** At $4.99, the impulse-buy threshold is low. At $9.99+, you need to justify with significantly more features.

4. **Revenue vs. volume trade-off:** $4.99 × 1,000 purchases = $4,999 (minus 30% store fee = $3,499 net). A subscription at $4.99/month needs 700+ active subscribers to match that in year one — much harder for a new app.

5. **Upgrade your current plan:** Your $1.99 price is below market. $4.99 is defensible and standard.

### Alternative: Hybrid Approach

Consider offering both:
- **One-time purchase:** $4.99 (ad-free + themes)
- **Subscription option:** $0.99/week or $19.99/year (adds cloud sync, Apple Watch companion, workout programs in V2)

This lets users choose their model. But **launch with one-time first** — simpler to implement, easier to market, and you can always add a subscription tier later.

- Setting up a timer (speed-ran, 5 sec)

## 7. KEY INSIGHTS & STRATEGIC RECOMMENDATIONS

### The Single Biggest Opportunity

**Background correctness is your killer feature.** Across every major competitor, the #1 complaint is "timer stops when screen turns off" or "timer is wrong after I lock my phone." You've already solved this. This should be front and center in your marketing:

> "The only round timer that keeps perfect time — even with your screen off."

### The Second Biggest Opportunity

**Users hate subscriptions for timer apps.** The market is moving toward subscriptions (Intervals Pro, SmartWOD, Interval Timer HIIT), and users are pushing back. A one-time purchase at $4.99 is a strong differentiator. Lead with it:

> "Pay once. No subscription. No monthly fees. Ever."

### The Third Biggest Opportunity

**Visual design matters more than you think.** Most timer apps look like they were built in 2016. Your blueprint design language, 10 themes, and attention to detail (reduce-motion, hairline rules, pulse animations) signal "premium" — which justifies the paid upgrade and builds trust for the download.

### Brand Name Recommendation

**Go with Bokta.** Reasons:
- Coined/fanciful = strongest trademark position
- Percussive [b]+[k] fits boxing/round timer brand
- Short (5 chars), memorable, easy to spell
- Tikbel sounds like "tickle" (wrong connotation for a tough sport)
- Kobal is too close to "cobalt" (could confuse with the element/material)

### Launch Strategy

1. **Soft launch in one market first** (UK — your home market, easier to get initial reviews from friends/network)
2. **Get 20–50 reviews at launch** (ask friends, Reddit r/homefitness, r/boxing, MMA forums, CrossFit communities)
3. **Use Apple Search Ads and Google App Campaigns** to accelerate initial downloads (budget $50–100/day for first 2 weeks)
4. **Monitor conversion rate** — if below 20%, A/B test screenshots immediately
5. **Update listing every 2–4 weeks** in the first 3 months to signal active maintenance to the store algorithms
